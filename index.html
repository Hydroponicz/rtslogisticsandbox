RECLAMATION FRONT

Valdoria, present day. The civil war wrecked the country's industry. Whoever restores it — and keeps the trucks rolling — wins the war.

YOUR JOB

Secure sites with infantry → bring engineers + stone to repair them → civilian crews commute in to run them → haul stone to the concrete plant and concrete to where it is needed → automate the hauling with NPC drivers → fortify, raid the enemy's network, and destroy the enemy HQ.
Cargo is physical: every crate sits somewhere on the map and only moves on a truck.

CONTROLS

WASD walk / drive · Mouse aim · LMB fire
E enter / exit truck
F on foot: call nearby troops to follow · in truck: board them
G on foot: send followers to cursor · in truck: unload passengers
Q cycle who G sends (all / infantry / engineers)
123 load 5 stone / concrete / supplies · Shift+ unload
R in truck at a site: mark route start, then route end
B cycle blueprint (depot / bunker), LMB place, Esc cancel
RMB inspect site or truck · Wheel zoom
P pause · [ ] game speed
